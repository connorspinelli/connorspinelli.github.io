AI Agent Pipeline for Release Documentation

Problem
Every product release has to make it from Jira tickets into user guides and release notes, and every claim in those docs has to trace back to its source.
What I did
Built a two-stage Python agent pipeline (13 scripts, 282 tests, 37 design controls) that drafts user-guide and release-note updates from Jira release data. I packaged 6 reusable agent skills and 2 MCP connectors (Paligo, Jira, Confluence) behind an installer and operator guide, so teammates who aren't engineers can run them.
Result
Drafts come out with 100% of claims cited. I also launched a 17-page internal AI hub that a scheduled agent audits every week, and brought a 100-page product user guide to Section 508 / WCAG 2.0 AA with zero alt-text failures.

Medical deviceJunior Design · Spring 2026

Wrist Support for Radial Artery Catheter Insertion

Problem
ICU clinicians need the wrist held at the right extension angle while they place and secure a radial artery catheter. If the wrist moves, the catheter can dislodge and pressure readings become unreliable.
What I did
With a 5-person BME team, I redesigned an existing support so it could be mass manufactured. I led concept generation and prototyping through several 3D-printed iterations, and we added adjustable wrist extension (a plunger spring pin on a lathe-turned hinge pin) and a catheter securement attachment.
Result
We carried it through fabrication, and I CNC machined the final parts from Delrin.

Rehab roboticsHuman Robotics Lab · Spring 2025

MRI-Compatible Support for the StretchWrist Robot

Problem
The lab's wrist rehab robot runs stroke-patient studies inside an MRI scanner. Patients' arms have to be supported in the bore, and nothing ferromagnetic can go near the magnet.
What I did
Designed an adjustable support in SolidWorks, sourced non-ferrous materials (brass and plywood), built it, and checked its fit against the scanner bore.
Result
The adjustability cuts down how much patients have to be maneuvered during setup. I also supported a PhD candidate's stroke-patient trials alongside clinical staff.
  • SolidWorks
  • MRI safety
  • Material selection
  • Woodworking

How much backpack is too much?

Graduated loading and sagittal-plane gait kinematics, n = 15

Fifteen participants walked carrying 0%, 10%, and 20% of their body weight. Mean trunk lean went from 7.96° to 12.07° at 20%, but people adapted so differently that the group-level effect wasn't significant.

I'm a senior in the University of Delaware's Biomedical Engineering Honors program, minoring in Biomechanical Engineering, and I'll finish a 4+1 MS in Robotics in May 2028.
