Wrist Support for Catheter Insertion

Problem
If the wrist moves during radial artery catheter placement in the ICU, the catheter can dislodge and pressure readings become unreliable.
What I did
Redesigned an existing support for mass manufacture with a 5-person team. I led concepts and 3D-printed prototypes, and we added adjustable wrist extension and a catheter securement attachment.
